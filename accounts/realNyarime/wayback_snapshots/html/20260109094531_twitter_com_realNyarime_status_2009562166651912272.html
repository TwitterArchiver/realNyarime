关于giffgaff ToS中的2.9条款

原文是
2.9. You may only use our services in the EU and selected destinations for periodic travel, like holidays or short breaks. If you’re not genuinely using our services for periodic travel we may have to charge you for, or suspend you from, using our services in the EU and selected destinations.
实际上仔细看2.9为什么一直强调欧盟(EU)，什么又叫genuinely using our services还有为什么charge和suspend会用or并列？

原来giffgaff为欧盟区提供了免费的漫游服务， 并且附带了公平使用条款: https://t.co/mC9ZaF30Ka
Your plan’s calls and texts allowance can be used in the EU and selected destinations (we call this the EU zone) just like at home and at no extra cost.

There’s a fair use limit on data of 5GB. If you go over it’ll cost 10p/MB. If you need more data you can buy a travel data add-on (more info below), or start a new plan early which will give you another 5GB allowance

这里的EU and selected destinations和2.9的描述恰好一致。所以说2.9实际上是用来规范EU and selected destinations免费漫游的合理使用的(旅游用途)。

关于这点giffgaff母公司O2的TOS里也说的很明白:
You can only use our services for periodic travel (like holidays or short breaks) in our Europe Zone (excluding the UK). Otherwise we may charge you or have to suspend you from using our services in our Europe Zone (excluding the UK). If this has happened to you and you think it might be a mistake, call 202 from your mobile or 0344 809 0202 from any other Phone.
You can use your O2 Pay As You Go phone in our Europe Zone at no extra cost for periodic travel – such as holidays or short breaks. Otherwise, we may charge you or have to suspend you from using our services in our Europe Zone (excluding the UK

不过话又说回来按公平使用条款的说法:
What to avoid:
Using our services for the first time outside of the UK, using a large volume of your allowance (be that text, calls or data) in the EU and our other selected destinations (excluding the UK), or using our services and travelling within the EU and our other selected destinations (excluding the UK) for prolonged periods which don’t follow reasonable consumer holiday and travel patterns and behaviour. If you use our services outside the UK in the EU and our other selected destinations for 63 or more days in any four-month period and you cannot demonstrate prevailing use or presence in the UK this is likely to be deemed to be an unfair use of our services.
首次不在英国激活或者漫超过63天即触犯条款。
We also we reserve the right to; (i) investigate your usage, (ii) impose Network protection controls (which may reduce your speed of connection) (iii) suspend you from using our services, (iv) disconnect your SIM card at any time (v) transfer you to a plan with a reduced data allowance and/or (vi) prevent you from purchasing further plans.
